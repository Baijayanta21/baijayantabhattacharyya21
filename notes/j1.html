\(j = 1\) Representation

For \(j=1\) the Lie algebra basis elements are given by : \begin {equation} J_x = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}}\left [\begin {matrix}0 & 1 & 0\\1 & 0 & 1\\0 & 1 & 0\end {matrix}\right ],\quad J_y = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}}\left [\begin {matrix}0 & - i & 0\\i & 0 & - i\\0 & i & 0\end {matrix}\right ], \quad J_z = \left [\begin {matrix}1 & 0 & 0\\0 & 0 & 0\\0 & 0 & -1\end {matrix}\right ] \end {equation}

We can write unit vector \(\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\) in terms of polar angles \((\psi ,\phi )\) as follows : \begin {equation} \hat {\mathbfit {n}} = \left [\begin {matrix}\sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\phi \right )}\\\sin {\left (\phi \right )} \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\\\cos {\left (\psi \right )}\end {matrix}\right ] \end {equation}

Using these forms we now evaluate the following : \begin {align*} \hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}} &= \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\phi \right )}\, J_x + \sin {\left (\phi \right )} \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\, J_y + \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\,J_z\\[6pt] &= \frac {\sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\phi \right )}}{\sqrt {2}}\left [\begin {matrix}0 & 1 & 0\\1 & 0 & 1\\0 & 1 & 0\end {matrix}\right ] + \frac {\sin {\left (\phi \right )} \sin {\left (\psi \right )}}{\sqrt {2}}\,\left [\begin {matrix}0 & - i & 0\\i & 0 & - i\\0 & i & 0\end {matrix}\right ] + \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\,\left [\begin {matrix}1 & 0 & 0\\0 & 0 & 0\\0 & 0 & -1\end {matrix}\right ]\\[6pt] &= \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}}\,\left [\begin {matrix}\sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & 0\\e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & 0 & e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\\0 & e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & - \sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\end {matrix}\right ] \end {align*}

\sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\end {matrix}\right ]\\[6pt] &= \frac {1}{2}\,\left [\begin {matrix}\sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} + 2 \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} & \sqrt {2} e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & e^{- 2 i \phi } \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\\\sqrt {2} e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & 2 \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} & - \sqrt {2} e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\\e^{2 i \phi } \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} & - \sqrt {2} e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} + 2 \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\end {matrix}\right ]\\[6pt] &= \frac {1}{2}\,\left [\begin {matrix}1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} & \sqrt {2} e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & e^{- 2 i \phi } \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\\\sqrt {2} e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & 2 \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} & - \sqrt {2} e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\\e^{2 i \phi } \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} & - \sqrt {2} e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & 1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\end {matrix}\right ] \end {align*}

Where we have used the fact that \(\sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} = 1\). Once again : \begin {align*} R = \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^3 &= \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )\left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^2\\[6pt] &= \frac {1}{2\sqrt {2}}\,\left [\begin {matrix}\sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & 0\\e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & 0 & e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\\0 & e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & - \sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\end {matrix}\right ]\times \\[15pt]&\hspace {2cm}\left [\begin {matrix}1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} & \sqrt {2} e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & e^{- 2 i \phi } \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\\\sqrt {2} e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & 2 \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} & - \sqrt {2} e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\\e^{2 i \phi } \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} & - \sqrt {2} e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & 1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\end {matrix}\right ]\\[6pt] &= \frac {1}{2\sqrt {2}}\,\left [\begin {matrix} R_{11} & R_{12} & R_{13}\\ R_{21} & R_{22} & R_{23}\\ R_{31} & R_{32} & R_{33}\\ \end {matrix}\right ] \end {align*}

Where : \begin {align*} R_{11} &= \left [\sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\times \left [1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\right ] + \sqrt {2} \sin ^2{\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\\[4pt] &= \sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\left [1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} + \sin ^2{\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\\[4pt] &= 2\sqrt {2}\cos {\left (\psi \right )}\\[15pt] R_{12}&= \left [\sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\times \left [\sqrt {2} e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\right ] + 2\,e^{- i \phi } \sin ^{3}{\left (\psi \right )}\\[4pt] &=2\,e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos ^2{\left (\psi \right )}+ 2\,e^{- i \phi } \sin ^{3}{\left (\psi \right )}\\[4pt] &=2\,e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \left [\cos ^2{\left (\psi \right )}+\sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\\[4pt] &=2\,e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\\[15pt] R_{13}&= \sqrt {2}\, e^{- 2 i \phi } \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}- \sqrt {2} e^{- 2 i \phi } \sin ^2{\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} = 0\\[15pt] R_{21}&= \left [e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\times \left [1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\right ] + \left [e^{-i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\times \left [e^{2 i \phi } \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\\[4pt] &= e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\left [1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} + \sin ^2{\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\\[4pt] &= 2e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\\[15pt] R_{22}&= \left [e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\times \left [\sqrt {2} e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\right ] + \left [e^{-i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\times \left [-\sqrt {2} e^{ i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\\[4pt] &=\sqrt {2} \sin ^2{\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} - \sqrt {2} \sin ^2{\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} = 0\\[15pt] R_{23}&= \left [e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\times \left [e^{-2 i \phi } \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\right ] + \left [e^{-i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\times \left [1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\\[4pt] &=e^{-i \phi } \sin ^3{\left (\psi \right )} + e^{-i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\left [1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\\[4pt] &= e^{-i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \left [\sin ^2{\left (\psi \right )} + 1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\\[4pt] &= 2e^{-i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\\[15pt] R_{31} &= \left [e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\times \left [\sqrt {2} e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\right ] + \left [- \sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\right ] \times \left [e^{2 i \phi } \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\\[4pt] &= \sqrt {2}\, e^{2 i \phi } \sin ^2{\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} - \sqrt {2}\, e^{2 i \phi } \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\sin ^2{\left (\psi \right )} = 0\\[15pt] R_{32} &= \left [e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\times \left [2 \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\right ] + \left [- \sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\right ] \times \left [- \sqrt {2} e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\\[4pt] &=2\,e^{i \phi } \sin ^3{\left (\psi \right )} + 2\,e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos ^2{\left (\psi \right )}\\[4pt] &=2\,e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\left [\sin ^2{\left (\psi \right )}+\cos ^2{\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\\[4pt] &= 2\,e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\\[15pt] R_{33} &= \left [e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\times \left [-\sqrt {2} e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\right ] + \left [- \sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\right ] \times \left [1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\right ]\\[4pt] &= -\sqrt {2}\, \sin ^2{\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} - Inserting which gives us : \begin {align*} R &= \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^3\\ &= \frac {1}{2\sqrt {2}}\,\left [\begin {matrix} R_{11} & R_{12} & R_{13}\\ R_{21} & R_{22} & R_{23}\\ R_{31} & R_{32} & R_{33}\\ \end {matrix}\right ]\\[4pt] &= \frac {1}{2\sqrt {2}}\,\left [\begin {matrix} 2\sqrt {2}\cos {\left (\psi \right )} & 2\,e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & 0 \\ 2e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & 0 & 2e^{-i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\\ 0 & 2\,e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} &-2\sqrt {2}\,\cos {\left (\psi \right )} \end {matrix}\right ]\\[4pt] &= \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}}\,\left [\begin {matrix}\sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & 0\\e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & 0 & e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\\0 & e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & - \sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\end {matrix}\right ] = \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right ) \end {align*}

We now have the following identity : \begin {equation} \boxed {\left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^3 = \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )} \end {equation} Which gives us the following relation : \begin {align} \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^{2m+1} = \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )\\[3pt] \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^{2m} = \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^2 \end {align}

Where \(m \in \mathbb {Z}_{>0}\), i.e. \(m = \{1,2,3,\dots \}\).

We now are in a position to write the representation of any element in SU(2) corresponding to \(j=1\) representation., which is given by : \begin {equation} \rho (g) = \textbf {exp}\left [-i\theta \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )\right ] \label {eq:rg} \end {equation} Expanding Eq. 4 gives : \begin {align*} \rho (g) &= \mathbb {1}_{3\times 3} - i\theta \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right ) + \frac {\left [i\theta \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )\right ]^2}{2!} - \frac {\left [i\theta \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )\right ]^3}{3!} + \cdots \\[10pt] &= \mathbb {1}_{3\times 3} + \sum _{n = 1}^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (-i\theta \right )^n}{n!} \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^n\\[10pt] &= \mathbb {1}_{3\times 3} + \left [\sum _{p = 0}^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (-i\theta \right )^{2p+1}}{(2p+1)!}\,\left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^{(2p+1)}\right ] + \left [\sum _{q = 1}^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (-i\theta \right )^{2q}}{(2q)!}\,\left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^{2q}\right ]\\[10pt] &= \mathbb {1}_{3\times 3} + \left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )\, \left [\sum _{p = 0}^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (-i\theta \right )^{2p+1}}{(2p+1)!}\,\right ]+\left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^2\, \left [\sum _{q = 1}^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (-i\theta \right )^{2q}}{(2q)!}\right ] \end {align*}

We now evaluate these two sums one by one. First : \begin {align*} \sum _{p = 0}^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (-i\theta \right )^{2p+1}}{(2p+1)!} &= -i\theta + \sum _{p = 1}^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (-i\theta \right )^{2p+1}}{(2p+1)!}\\[7pt] &=-i\theta + \sum _{p = 1,3,5,\dots }^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (-i\theta \right )^{2p+1}}{(2p+1)!} + \sum _{p = 2,4,6,\dots }^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (-i\theta \right )^{2p+1}}{(2p+1)!}\\[7pt] &=-i\theta + i\,\left (\sum _{p = 1,3,5,\dots }^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (\theta \right )^{2p+1}}{(2p+1)!}\right ) -i\left ( \sum _{p = 2,4,6,\dots }^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (\theta \right )^{2p+1}}{(2p+1)!}\right )\\[7pt] &= -i\theta + i\left [\frac {\theta ^3}{3!} +\frac {\theta ^7}{7!} +\cdots \right ] - i\left [\frac {\theta ^5}{5!} +\frac {\theta ^9}{9!} +\cdots \right ]\\[7pt] &= -i\,\left [\theta - \frac {\theta ^3}{3!} + \frac {\theta ^5}{5!} - \frac {\theta ^7}{7!} + \frac {\theta ^9}{9!} -\cdots \right ]\\[7pt] &= -i\sin \theta \end {align*}

Where we have used the following arguments : \((-i)^{2m} = (-1)^{2m}\cdot (i)^{2m} = i^{2m} = -1\,\text {if}\, m\, \text {is odd, and}\, 1\,\text {if}\, m\, \text {is even}\). Shifting our result, we can say \((-i)^{2r+1} = (-i)^{2r}\cdot (-i) = i\,\text {if}\, r\, \text {is odd, and}\, -i\,\text {if}\, r\, \text {is even.}\)

The next sum is evaluated as follows : \begin {align*} \sum _{q=1}^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (-i\theta \right )^{2q}}{(2q)!} &=\sum _{q = 1,3,5,\dots }^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (-i\theta \right )^{2q}}{(2q)!} + \sum _{q = 2,4,6,\dots }^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (-i\theta \right )^{2q}}{(2q)!}\\[7pt] &=-1\,\left (\sum _{q = 1,3,5,\dots }^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (\theta \right )^{2q}}{(2q)!}\right ) +1\left ( \sum _{q = 2,4,6,\dots }^{\infty }\,\frac {\left (\theta \right )^{2q}}{(2q)!}\right )\\[7pt] &= -1\left [\frac {\theta ^2}{2!} +\frac {\theta ^6}{6!} +\cdots \right ] +1\left [\frac {\theta ^4}{4!} +\frac {\theta ^8}{8!} +\cdots \right ]\\[7pt] &= -\frac {\theta ^2}{2!} + \frac {\theta ^4}{4!} - \frac {\theta ^6}{6!} + \frac {\theta ^8}{8!} -\cdots \\[7pt] &= 1-1 -\frac {\theta ^2}{2!} + \frac {\theta ^4}{4!} - \frac {\theta ^6}{6!} + \frac {\theta ^8}{8!} -\cdots \\[7pt] &= -1 + \left [1-\frac {\theta ^2}{2!} + \frac {\theta ^4}{4!} - \frac {\theta ^6}{6!} + \frac {\theta ^8}{8!} -\cdots \right ]\\[7pt] &= -1 + \cos \theta \end {align*}

Which gives us the representation as : \begin {equation} \boxed {\rho (g) = \mathbb {1}_{3\times 3} -i\left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )\,\sin \theta + (\cos \theta -1)\,\left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^2} \end {equation} Which is quite nice form. Substituting the form of \(\left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )\) and \(\left (\hat {\mathbfit {n}}\cdot \vec {\mathbfit {J}}\right )^2\) in this we get : \begin {align*} \rho (g) &= \mathbb {1}_{3\times 3} -\frac {i\sin \theta }{\sqrt {2}}\,\left [\begin {matrix}\sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & 0\\e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & 0 & e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )}\\0 & e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} & - \sqrt {2} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\end {matrix}\right ]+\\[10pt] &\frac {(\cos \theta -1)}{2}\,\left [\begin {matrix}1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} & \sqrt {2} e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & e^{- 2 i \phi } \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\\\sqrt {2} e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & 2 \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} & - \sqrt {2} e^{- i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )}\\e^{2 i \phi } \sin ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )} & - \sqrt {2} e^{i \phi } \sin {\left (\psi \right )} \cos {\left (\psi \right )} & 1 + \cos ^{2}{\left (\psi \right )}\end {matrix}\right ] \end {align*}
