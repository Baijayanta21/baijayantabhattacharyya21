01 / ABOUT
About Me

A physicist between ideas and data.

An academic profile built around cosmology and computational analysis.

My Research At a glance

I am a PhD student in the Department of Physics at IIT Kharagpur, working with Prof. Somnath Bharadwaj.

My research focuses on theoretical cosmology and astrophysics, particularly the redshifted 21-cm signal of neutral hydrogen and its use as a probe of the early Universe.

Alongside theoretical work, I work with radio-interferometric observations and computational pipelines, including data associated with the Murchison Widefield Array.

02 / RESEARCH
About My Research

Questions written in radio waves.

The research programme connects cosmological theory with the observational problem of extracting a faint 21-cm signal from radio data.

My Research At a glance

21-cm Cosmology

Neutral hydrogen as a tracer of cosmic structure and the thermal and ionization history of the intergalactic medium.

Epoch of Reionization

Understanding the transition from a largely neutral intergalactic medium to the ionized Universe we observe today.

Radio Interferometry

Calibration, simulations and analysis methods for extracting cosmological information from low-frequency radio observations.

Scientific Computing

Numerical methods, Python-based analysis and visualisation for large scientific datasets and cosmological calculations.
